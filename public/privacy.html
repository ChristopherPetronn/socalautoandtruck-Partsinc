Privacy Policy

How SoCal Auto & Truck Parts, Inc. handles website and customer information.

Effective Date: Date of publication. This document is a business policy draft and should be reviewed against actual company procedures before publication.

1. Information We Collect

Information may include contact information such as name, business name, email, telephone and business address; vehicle information such as year, make, model, engine, VIN if voluntarily provided and application details; parts request information such as part number, category, quantity and purchase-order reference; business account information; transaction information such as quote history, invoices and order details; and technical information such as IP address, browser, device type, operating system, pages visited, timestamps and basic security logs.

2. How Information Is Collected

Information may be collected when a visitor submits a form, requests a quotation, contacts the Company by telephone or email, opens a business account or places an order. Limited technical information may be collected automatically by hosting, security and website services actually enabled.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to respond to customer inquiries, identify vehicle and part requirements, verify parts compatibility, prepare quotes and order confirmations, process approved business account requests, coordinate pickup or delivery, maintain records, communicate about backorders or order issues, detect fraud, improve the website and meet legal or contractual obligations.

4. Information Sharing

Information may be shared as reasonably necessary with website hosting and email providers, parts manufacturers and suppliers, authorized distributors and sourcing partners, shipping carriers, payment providers where applicable, professional advisers, government authorities where legally required, a lawful business successor or other parties when the customer expressly authorizes disclosure.

5. No Sale of Customer Lists

The Company does not offer customer contact lists for sale. Information may be processed by service providers acting on the Company’s behalf as necessary to operate the website and conduct business.

6. Cookies and Website Technologies

The website may use essential cookies for security, session management and functionality. Analytics or advertising technologies should be used only if actually implemented and disclosed as required by applicable law. Visitors may manage cookie settings through their browsers.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle inquiries, quotes, business accounts, transactions, returns, warranty matters, accounting, disputes and legal obligations. Retention periods vary according to information type and applicable law. Information no longer required may be securely deleted or disposed of, subject to lawful retention and backup cycles.

8. Data Security

The Company uses reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information handled. No online transmission or data storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Customers should not submit passwords, bank login credentials, payment-card security codes or unnecessary sensitive personal information through general website forms.

9. California Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and business eligibility, California residents may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information and to receive information about certain data practices. Certain exemptions, verification requirements and legal exceptions may apply, including for records maintained to complete transactions or comply with legal obligations. Privacy requests can be submitted using the contact information below.

10. Third-Party Websites

The website may contain links to suppliers, manufacturers, carriers and other third-party websites. Those services have independent privacy policies. The Company does not control their separate information practices.

11. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for business customers and automotive parts purchasers and is not directed to children under 13. The Company does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through this website.

12. Policy Changes

This Policy may be updated as the website, business operations and legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
