Effective Date: Date of publication. This document is a business policy draft and should be reviewed against actual company procedures before publication.

1. General Policy

Parts orders are often vehicle-specific or sourced according to a customer’s exact requirements. Customers should verify part numbers, vehicle applications, quantities and specifications before approving an order. A return request must be submitted to the Company for review. No return should be shipped or delivered without prior instructions or return authorization where required. Nothing in this Policy waives rights that cannot legally be waived.

2. Return Authorization

Customers should contact the Company before returning any product. Provide customer or business name, original invoice or order number, product description and part number, quantity, purchase date, reason for return, product condition and supporting photographs where appropriate. The Company may issue a return authorization and handling instructions. Receipt of a request does not automatically mean it has been approved.

3. Incorrect or Damaged Shipments

If an order appears damaged, incomplete or different from the confirmed order, contact the Company promptly. Provide photographs of the product, packaging, shipping labels and relevant order documentation where available. The Company may review the shipment, carrier records, supplier documentation and original order to determine an appropriate correction, replacement, credit or refund under the applicable terms and law.

4. Incorrect Part Selection

Customers are responsible for providing accurate vehicle and part information. If a product does not fit because incorrect or incomplete vehicle information was supplied, the return request will be reviewed under the applicable order terms and return authorization process. A parts specialist may request the VIN, original part number or additional vehicle details to investigate the issue.

5. Product Condition

Approved returns generally require the product to be in the condition specified in the return authorization. Products that have been installed, used, altered, damaged through misuse or rendered unsalable may not be eligible for return, except where required by law or an applicable warranty. Packaging, manuals, accessories and original documentation should be retained where reasonably possible.

6. Special-Order Products

Special-order, non-stock, custom-sourced or vehicle-specific products may be subject to supplier restrictions or cancellation costs. Eligibility for return must be confirmed before purchase whenever possible. Any applicable non-returnable condition or supplier charge must be disclosed in the quote, invoice or written order terms and must comply with law.

7. Electrical and Sensitive Components

Electrical, electronic or sensitive components may be subject to manufacturer testing, handling and return restrictions. The return eligibility of a particular item depends on the supplier’s rules, product condition, written order terms and applicable law. Do not assume that an opened or installed component can automatically be returned.

8. Core Charges

Some replacement components may involve a refundable core deposit or core charge. Where applicable, the amount, qualifying core condition, return instructions and deadline will be stated in the invoice or order documents. Core credits are subject to inspection and compliance with the applicable terms.

9. Shipping and Restocking Charges

Any restocking fee, return shipping cost, freight charge or supplier-imposed cost must be disclosed in the applicable quote, invoice or return authorization and comply with law. The Company will not invent or apply an undisclosed charge.

10. Manufacturer Warranties

Products may be covered by a manufacturer’s warranty. Warranty claims are governed by the manufacturer’s documentation and the specific product terms. The Company may help coordinate documentation or supplier communication where appropriate, but no independent warranty is created unless expressly stated in writing.

11. Order Cancellations

Requests to cancel an order should be submitted as soon as possible. Cancellation may depend on whether the order has been accepted, picked, shipped, specially sourced or committed to a supplier. Any cancellation fee or non-cancelable special-order cost must be disclosed and permitted by the applicable written terms and law.

12. Refunds and Credits

If a return is approved, the remedy may be a refund, replacement, exchange or account credit, depending on the transaction documents, product condition and applicable law. Approved refunds will generally be processed using the original payment method where practicable. Business account adjustments may be reflected as invoice credits when agreed.

13. Billing Disputes

Customers who believe an invoice contains an error should notify the Company with the invoice number, relevant line item and supporting documentation. The Company will review the information and communicate the outcome or request additional details.

14. How to Request a Return or Refund

Contact SoCal Auto & Truck Parts, Inc. at (657) 274-3661 or contact@socalautoandtruckpartsinc.com. Include your business name, contact information, invoice number, product details, quantity and reason for the request. Do not send payment-card numbers, passwords or bank login details.

15. No Waiver of Legal Rights

Nothing in this Policy restricts a customer’s rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded under applicable California or federal law. If a provision conflicts with mandatory law, the law controls.

16. Policy Updates

The Company may update this Policy as its operations, supplier requirements or legal obligations change. Updated text and the effective date will be published on this page.
